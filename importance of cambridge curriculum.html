Importance of Cambridge Curriculum in Bhutan The Cambridge Curriculum helps students develop skills that are useful in both school and everyday life. It encourages students to **think independently, ask questions, communicate clearly, and solve problems**. Students can learn through practical activities instead of depending only on memorization. The curriculum also gives importance to **technology and innovation**. Students can improve their digital skills and learn how technology can be used for education and future careers. Another important part is **international education**. Cambridge learning can help students understand ideas from around the world while continuing to respect **Bhutanese culture, traditions, values, and environment**. The curriculum also encourages **teamwork and collaboration**. Students can work together on activities and projects, share ideas, and learn from one another. Overall, the Cambridge Curriculum aims to prepare Bhutanese students to become **confident, creative, responsible, and skilled learners** who can contribute positively to Bhutan and the wider world.
